Plant Care Guide

Simple care tips for healthy plants

Use these standard tips for indoor plants, succulents, herbs and flowering plants.

💧

Watering

Check the top soil before watering. Indoor plants usually prefer watering once in 5 to 7 days, while succulents need less water.

☀️

Sunlight

Most indoor plants prefer bright indirect sunlight. Flowering plants need more sunlight to bloom properly.

🪴

Soil

Use well-draining potting mix. Succulents need sandy soil. Herbs grow better in compost-rich garden soil.

🌱

Fertilizer

Apply organic compost or balanced liquid fertilizer once a month during the active growth period.

🦠

Disease Care

Remove yellow or infected leaves early. Avoid overwatering and keep good airflow around the plant.

✂️

Pruning

Trim dried leaves and weak branches to encourage fresh growth and maintain shape.
